# Replacement cap forecast

A supplier said the replacement cap **may** arrive on Friday. Receipt has not
been confirmed. The note records a possibility, not a completed repair or a
promise. There is no established effective date for renewed loan availability.

An accepted decision, an action taken, and an observed outcome would each need
their own record. No actor has approved a loan in this example.
