# Atelier Dev Day run sheet

Rehearsal target: Wednesday 7 October 2026. In-person use: Thursday 8 October 2026, Asia/Makassar. This is a planned protocol; no participant result has been observed here.

Before the event, rehearse the exact published version on a clean consumer. Record package version, integrity, tested Mac/Node, normal installed entry, public example and owning source route. Bring the pinned release and verified quickstart. A local tarball run and registry installation are separate records. If the full guided journey is unavailable, disclose that limit and use only the supported baseline exercise; do not present it as the complete delivery.

Allow roughly 25–35 minutes for the exercise, adapting to the participant. Explain that their words, disagreement and decision to pause remain theirs. Ask whether they want their feedback recorded, use a participant code rather than a name, and preserve exact words only within the agreed note custody. They may decline or stop at any point.

1. Ask what the participant currently thinks the source relationship means. Record their explanation and confidence before showing the finding. Ask what evidence would change their view.
2. Open the installed public example through its normal entry. Let them locate the source passage and supporting evidence. Ask them to explain the finding in their own words, including what the tool cannot establish.
3. Invite a contribution relevant to the example. Preserve their exact original words. Let them inspect any proposed interpretation and choose agreement, disagreement, perspective-only or pause. Do not reinterpret a pause as acceptance.
4. Let them inspect the proposed revision and preview. Ask what will change, who owns the write, and what should remain unchanged. Apply only through the example's existing owning route after their explicit choice. Saving words and applying a source correction are different actions.
5. Verify the exact accepted readback, reopen through the normal entry, and rerun the affected check. Let the participant explain what changed and what remains uncertain. Preserve a historical finding/context rather than relabeling it current.
6. Ask what was difficult, whether the contribution felt meaningful, and whether the result was useful. Record an exact quotation if they agreed, elapsed time and assistance used. Separate their judgment from facilitator interpretation and automated results.

If a write is uncertain, preserve the original operation ID and full intent. Use the existing original-ID lookup/readback before any admitted identical retry. Keep the interface in an unconfirmed/checking state until readback resolves it. Do not create a new operation ID, erase history or show Saved early. If the owning route cannot resolve it, stop the write step and retain the trace for support.
