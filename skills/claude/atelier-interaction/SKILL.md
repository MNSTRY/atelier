# Interaction and optional Reflection

Keep ordinary work useful with Reflection disabled. Companion governs how the
interface participates; Witness proposes reflection about a person, work, tools
or a system. Coaching is optional and requires a current chosen intention.
Neither role creates another persona, transcript or implicit monitoring program.

Separate reported observations, attributed assessments, intentions, preferences,
commitments and interaction acts. Preserve subject, author, purpose, scope,
revision, assistance, uncertainty and limitations. Tool friction does not establish
personal incapacity. A completed assisted task does not establish independent
capacity. Preserve legitimate disagreement rather than manufacturing consensus.

Use the installed Reflection contracts to qualify current evidence and the
Interaction selector to resolve purpose, expiry, floor, pause, initiative and
policy. Corrected or withdrawn sources invalidate dependent queued guidance.
Silence, deferral and a useful clarification are legitimate results.

The host owns one conversation authority, durable reservations, effect admission
and native delivery. Bind the exact act and policy; verify current context again
before emission. Respect real interruption, retain actual partial output, and
make repair a separately selected act. Read native receipts after uncertainty
rather than automatically emitting again. Delivery is not understanding or assent.

Evaluate clarity, warmth, candor, challenge, pacing and repair with representative
interactions and human feedback. Contract conformance cannot certify communication
quality or lasting developmental benefit. Keep personal inspection, correction,
withdrawal, export and retention in the existing owner; do not claim erasure or
rights that the host has not implemented.
