# Independent inquiry prompts

Fill in the question, purpose, definitions, scope, time horizon, audience and
constraints in every prompt. Select lenses for the actual question; six is an
example, not a required quota.

1. **Direct evidence:** What do the strongest primary sources establish? Separate
   observations, estimates, interpretations and claims that remain unverified.
2. **Alternative explanations:** What other accounts fit the observations? State
   the evidence that would distinguish them and seek that evidence.
3. **Mechanisms and context:** How could the result arise? Which assumptions,
   institutions, incentives or conditions does that account require?
4. **Variation:** Where does the pattern differ across settings, populations,
   periods or methods? Look for boundaries on any claimed generalization.
5. **Consequences:** What practical choices follow, with what tradeoffs and for
   whom? Separate evidenced outcomes from forecasts and value judgments.
6. **Disconfirmation:** Build the strongest evidence-backed challenge to the
   leading interpretation. Identify missing data and decisive next observations.

Append to each prompt:

> Return a standalone report with claim identifiers and a source ledger. Prefer
> primary material. Give titles, authors, dates, URLs or identifiers and precise
> locators where accessible. Distinguish source text, inference and uncertainty.
> Identify unavailable sources explicitly. Include evidence against your conclusion,
> search limitations and questions for follow-up. Do not manufacture citations.
