# Synthesis structure

- Decision or question and current scope.
- Coverage: planned inquiries, received reports, missing evidence and limits.
- Findings with claim IDs and report/source locators.
- Agreements, disagreements, source dependence and competing explanations.
- Implications separated from factual findings.
- Confidence rationale, boundary conditions and unresolved questions.
- Human review decisions and proposed next investigations.

Every major finding must be traceable through report and source identifiers.
A polished summary does not repair a broken evidence chain.
