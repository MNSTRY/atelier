# Synthesize research

Inventory the expected inquiries and the reports actually present. Keep raw
reports unchanged and give each a stable identifier and content digest through
the repository's existing intake mechanism. Identify missing or failed inquiries.

Use the existing evidence-review capability, or a disclosed manual equivalent,
to review consequential claims. Group reports by underlying sources before
counting corroboration. Separate consensus, independent support, shared-source
repetition, disagreement, and genuinely unresolved questions.

Use [the synthesis structure](references/synthesis.md). Retain contradictory
findings and the scope of each claim. Explain what changed from the initial
hypothesis and what new information could change the conclusion again.

Ask a human to validate the resulting interpretation before it becomes an
accepted canonical claim. The synthesis and its graph proposal can remain drafts
while unanswered questions are pursued.
