# Integration checklist

1. Inventory source reports and hashes; preserve originals.
2. Identify the destination namespace, audience, allowed node types and relations.
3. Reconcile duplicates using source identity and evidence, not title similarity.
4. Connect claims to reports and primary sources, including contradictory claims.
5. Keep unknown provenance and missing reports visible as gaps.
6. Validate the proposed documents with the destination's actual graph command.
7. Retrieve representative conclusions and follow their links back to evidence.
8. Record human acceptance separately from successful ingestion and validation.
