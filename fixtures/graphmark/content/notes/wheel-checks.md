# Wheel checks before a bicycle leaves the workshop

An invented community repair workshop keeps this note. Volunteers check a
repaired wheel in this order, because each check can undo the one before it.

| Order | Check | Why it comes here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Spoke tension, by ear and by hand | Truing a wheel with slack spokes only moves the wobble |
| 2 | Side-to-side true | Tension is now even enough for a true to hold |
| 3 | Hub bearings | A true wheel shows bearing play that a wobble hides |
| 4 | Tyre seated and pressure set | Seating last means the earlier work is not disturbed |

A volunteer who finds a fault at any step starts again from step 1.
