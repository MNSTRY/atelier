# Mark: the order of wheel checks

**What was marked.** The table in the wheel checks note (lines 16 to 21): four
checks in a fixed order, each with the reason it comes where it does.

**Why it was marked.** For the order and its reasons. Each check can undo the
one before it, and a fault sends the volunteer back to the start. Training
material that lists the same four checks in another order would lose the point.

**Provenance.** Made by the workshop's volunteers. Marked by the volunteer lead
on 12 January 2026. The edition is the note's bytes with the content digest
above, at the revision named in the target. Source: the note itself.
Interpretation: the sentence above about training material is the marker's
reading, not part of the note.

**What this mark does not do.** It does not adopt the order as the workshop's
rule, publish it or approve the note. The note's owner still decides that.

**Open interpretation (candidate, not an edge).** The same fault-restarts
pattern may apply to the brake checks. Not yet shown.
